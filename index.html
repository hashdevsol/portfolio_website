About

A developer who thinks in requests per second, not just pixels.

I started in 2021 patching a slow Express API for a logistics startup and never left the backend. Five years later I work across the whole MERN stack — React and Next.js on the front, Node, Express and MongoDB behind it — with a bias toward apps that have to stay up under real traffic.

Most of my work falls into three shapes: SaaS dashboards with roles, billing and audit trails; marketplaces where payments and search have to be correct; and rescue jobs where an existing codebase needs a performance pass before it buckles. I write tested code, document the handover, and leave clients with something their next developer can read.

Outside client work I maintain two small open-source packages for Mongoose pagination and review pull requests for a React component library.
